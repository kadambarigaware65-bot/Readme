📚 Study-Planner-CLI

✨ Study Planner CLI

Stay organized and manage your studies directly from your terminal! Study Planner CLI is a simple Python project that helps students plan their daily study sessions, list their subjects, and stay focused on their academic goals.

Students often struggle to manage multiple subjects, assignments, and exams. This lightweight Python program makes study planning easier by providing a simple way to organize daily tasks.

✨ Features

* 📝 Add Study Tasks: Create a list of subjects or study tasks.
* 📋 View Tasks: Display all your planned study activities.
* ✅ Task Management: Mark tasks as completed.
* 🎯 Stay Focused: Organize your study routine and improve productivity.
* 🐍 Simple Python Code: Easy to understand and beginner-friendly.
* 💻 Lightweight: Runs directly in the terminal without external libraries.
